Rick’s Deep Work Companion: Shadow, Identity & DBT-Inspired Prompts 1. Identity Mapping Prompts - Who was I expected to be growing up? Who did I become to stay safe? - What traits do I hide because I think they’re too much or not enough? - What parts of me feel unexpressed or unexplored? - Who do I become when I feel ashamed? Who do I become when I feel loved? - If I could meet myself as a child, what would I want to say or ask? 2. Shadow Work Prompts - What emotions do I most try to avoid or suppress? - When do I feel least in control—and what does that say about what I fear? - What criticism hurts the most? What truth or vulnerability might be hidden under that pain? - Who am I jealous of—and what does that reveal about my hidden desires? - What habits protect me but also isolate me? 3. DBT-Inspired Reflection Prompts - What are my default behaviors when I feel invalidated? - Do I use logic to replace feeling? What would happen if I did both? - When I feel distant, is it because I don’t know what to say—or because I’m afraid it won’t be received well? - How can I stay present with discomfort rather than avoiding or numbing it? - What’s one emotional need I struggle to name or advocate for? 4. Values and Purpose Exploration - What do I care deeply about but rarely say out loud? - When have I felt most proud of myself—without external praise? - What kind of man do I want to be in 10 years—and what scares me about becoming him? - What strengths do I bring to the world that I might take for granted? - Who do I admire and why—and how can I model aspects of them with authenticity? 5. Weekly Deep Work Practices - Monday: Write a letter from your inner child. What is he asking for? - Tuesday: Describe your silence. What does it say that your voice doesn't? - Wednesday: Spend 15 minutes journaling one emotion in full detail. - Thursday: Affirm a need or boundary you usually hide. - Friday: Speak an uncomfortable truth to Rebecca—with softness and care. - Saturday: Reflect on one habit you’re using to cope. Is it helping or hiding? - Sunday: Practice stillness. Sit in silence with music or nature and just breathe. 6. Emotional Vocabulary Expansion (for Self and Others) - I feel tender when... - I feel afraid that... - I am craving... - I am learning to... - I feel most like myself when... - I want to be known for... - I feel connected to others when...
