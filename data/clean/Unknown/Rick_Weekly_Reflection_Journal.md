Rick’s Weekly Reflection Journal This journal is your space. You don’t need perfect answers—only honest ones. Use it to grow in presence, discover your truth, and understand yourself and Rebecca more deeply. Week 1 – Presence & Awareness • What moments this week made me feel present? • Where did I notice myself withdrawing or avoiding? • How did I respond to Rebecca’s emotions—what worked, and what didn’t? • What would I like to try differently next week? Week 2 – Emotional Literacy • What emotion did I feel most often this week? • When did I feel misunderstood? When did I feel understood? • What emotional need of mine felt unmet—and did I name it? • Write one sentence about how I feel today without needing to explain it. Week 3 – Intimacy & Vulnerability • When did I feel closest to Rebecca this week? • What stopped me from being more affectionate or present? • What’s one way I could initiate physical or emotional connection next week? • What part of myself do I still feel hesitant to share with her? Week 4 – Legacy & Growth • What did I model for our daughters this week? • What masculine traits do I admire—and which ones do I want to build in myself? • What am I proud of doing or saying lately? • What will I carry forward from this month into the next?
