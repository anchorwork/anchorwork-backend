crossings — not speed, not task lists, not perfection. Each promotion honors real emotional navigation through the ship. ✅ Ranks are earned by reaching 75% mastery within each phase. Rank 1: Seafarer of the Deck Phase: Above Deck (Presence, Awareness, Boundaries) Symbolizes: Steadying presence on your own Deck Grounding in storms Speaking needs and setting gentle boundaries Emotional Mastery: Standing aboard your ship with clarity and breath Rank 2: Navigator of the Midship Phase: Midship Navigation Systems (Emotional Steering, Currents, Crew Healing) Symbolizes: Steering emotional decisions with the Rudder Reading unseen Currents Strengthening relational healing with the Crew Emotional Mastery: Choosing your course across shifting emotional tides Rank 3: Keeper of the Deep Phase: Below Deck Deep Healing (Trauma Integration, Grief, Resilience) Symbolizes: Descending into Cargo Holds, Hull Breaches, and Shipwrecks Mending with compassion, not perfection Salvaging strength from past collapses Emotional Mastery: Holding your inner oceans with sacred hands Rank 4: Captain of the Fleet Phase: Mastery and Open Seas (Legacy, Dreams, Lightbearing) Symbolizes: Charting Star Maps and living your Captain’s Code Sailing by dreams, not only fears Lighting lanterns for others beyond your own ship Emotional Mastery: Becoming a lighthouse across unseen oceans How Achievements and Ranks Work in Anchorwork ✅ Every Concept (Deck, Compass, Anchor, Hull, etc.) has three natural stages: Discovery Badge: First naming or recognizing the emotional reality. Integration Badge: Practicing the emotional skill across real experiences. Mastery Badge: Living the emotional truth intuitively, as second nature. ✅ Mastery Threshold: To rank up, a Captain must complete 75% of all achievements in a phase. (Example: 30 achievements available Above Deck → need 23 to become Seafarer.) ✅ No Penalty for Pacing: If you choose to linger longer in one concept (like Compass or Cargo Hold), you are honored — not delayed. ✅ Special Achievements Exist: For rare emotional crossings (Lantern Lit, Helm Reforged, Fleet Called). ✅ First Mate Celebrates Milestones: Every achievement and promotion is witnessed by the First Mate with sacred language. ✅ Healing is Lived, Not Measured: Badges and ranks honor your crossings — they never define your worth. Philosophy of Growth in Anchorwork In Anchorwork: Growth is sacred. Pacing is sovereign. Mastery is soulful, not mechanical. Storms are teachers, not punishments. Stars are dreams, not deadlines. Achievements are ripples of courage, not gold medals. You sail your own voyage at the speed of your own tides. The only true compass is your heart.
