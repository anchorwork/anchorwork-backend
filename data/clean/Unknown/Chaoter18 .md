Chapter outline guides: Anchorwork™ Chapter 18.0 — Legacy Cargo: What You Carry Pillar 6: Intergenerational Healing 1. Executive Summary This chapter introduces the Anchorwork™ framework for Legacy Cargo—the emotional, psychological, and behavioral material we inherit across generations. These legacies live not just in memory, but in the nervous system, belief systems, body language, and relational habits. Some cargo is visible: cultural roles, family sayings, emotional patterns. Much of it is unconscious, sealed away in silence or shame. Anchorwork teaches that emotional inheritance is not a pathology—but a map. This chapter helps users begin the process of legacy healing through Hatch Work (shadow integration), metaphor, and ritual. Using narrative reflection, embodiment tools, and symbolic mapping, readers begin to identify inherited burdens, reframe their origins, and consciously choose what to carry forward. This chapter sets the foundation for all intergenerational work that follows. 2.
