Here is the full Anchorwork Emotional Climate Summary: What Is Emotional Climate? In Anchorwork, your emotional weather is how you feel today. Your emotional climate is the long-term emotional ecosystem you live in — shaped by past experience, cultural inheritance, relationships, nervous system patterns, and identity. Climates are not permanent, but they tend to persist until intentionally shifted or rewired through ritual, relationship, and regulation. Why Emotional Climates Matter •They form the baseline for how you interpret new experiences •They influence your habits, relationships, resilience, and worldview •They can be inherited (Legacy Climates), created for survival, or consciously chosen •They determine whether your internal world feels like a safe harbor or a turbulent sea Anchorwork Emotional Climate Types Climate NameEmotional Nervous System Mapping Climates also correspond to long-term nervous system states: •Storm / Flood / Heated = Sympathetic (fight/flight) •Fog-Dense / Frozen / Burnt = Dorsal Vagal (shutdown, freeze) •Warm / Nourished / Sky-Wide = Ventral Vagal (regulation, connection) Seasonal Alignment Anchorwork links climates to the broader human and ecological cycle: •Spring Climates = Renewal, reinvention (e.g., Spring Breakthrough, Wind-Tossed) •Summer Climates = Expression, joy (e.g., Summer Radiance, Sky-Wide) •Autumn Climates = Grief, letting go (e.g., Autumn Drift, Tectonic) •Winter Climates = Rest, numbness, integration (e.g., Frozen Field, Burnt Terrain) First Mate Forecast Language Examples •“We’ve had several foggy mornings this week.
