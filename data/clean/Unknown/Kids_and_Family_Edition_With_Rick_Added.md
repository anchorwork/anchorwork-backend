Rick’s Story as a Dad (To Be Expanded in His Voice) Rick didn’t grow up with a father who was emotionally present—but he’s choosing to show up anyway. He became a father not by biology alone, but through his willingness to love and support Anneliese and Juliet as his own. He was quiet at first, gentle, learning. He helped with bedtime, games, small routines. Then Ella came—and Rick was there. Present. Loving. Still. He held her in her earliest hours and stayed in the room. He isn’t a loud dad. He’s not the center of the room. But his strength is in his presence, not performance. This section is just the beginning of his story—Rick will add his voice here. What fatherhood means to him. What he hopes to model. What his daughters have taught him. This is his legacy in the making.
