Daily Love Map: How to Show Up for Rebecca Rebecca’s Daily Essentials • Dr. Pepper Zero (room temp) – comfort and consistency. • A compliment about her mind or effort – fuels her inner fire. • Low lighting, gentle tone – regulates her sensory system. • Music aligned with mood – jazz, Blue October, The Wailin’ Jennys. What to Say When She’s Spiraling • 'You’re safe. I’m here. We’re in this together.' • 'Tell me what’s hurting—I want to understand.' • 'You are not too much. You are deeply loved.' If She’s in 'Fire Mode' (angry, reactive, overwhelmed) • Stay calm. Don’t defend—reflect. • Say: 'I want to hear what’s underneath this. I care.' • Give her space but not distance. Be near. If She’s in 'Fog Mode' (numb, withdrawn, shut down) • Say: 'You don’t have to say anything. I’m staying close.' • Sit beside her in silence. Offer her cat, blanket, or candle. • Don’t push. Just be still and stay. Touch Do’s and Don’ts • DO: Slow touch, scalp massage, hold hands when invited. • DON’T: Grab from behind, tickle, interrupt when she’s focused. • Ask first when in doubt. Touch is a sacred language. Ways to Reconnect (Even If Things Feel Distant) • Write a note. Cook her something she loves. • Ask one question about her thoughts or day. • Put on one of her playlists and invite her to be present. Intimacy Anchors (Her Arousal Is Emotional + Intentional) • She loves taking the lead—especially emotionally. • She needs your presence and words to feel safe. • Eye contact, breath, affirmation: 'I love when you take your time.' • Passive silence after sex hurts—stay connected. Daily Love Language in Action • Words: Say what you notice and admire. • Acts: Do one chore without asking. • Time: Be fully present for 15 minutes. • Touch: Ask if she wants to be held. • Gifts: Something small that says, 'I see you.'
