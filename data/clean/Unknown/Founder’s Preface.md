Founder’s Preface Rebecca Morris, Creator of Anchorwork™ In the quiet winter of 2024, my world changed. On December 8th, I lost my father, Adolph “Lee” Siggers — a Lieutenant Commander, a leader, a man of profound steadiness and wisdom. He was my first anchor — the one who taught me how to navigate life’s unpredictable seas with courage, clarity, and heart. In the wake of that loss, I entered what I now call my “storm season” — a period of deep reflection, grief, and transformation. Rather than allow myself to drift into despair, I chose to honor the lessons he gave me. I turned inward to navigate the emotional weather of my own soul — and outward, to formalize what I had long intuited about psychology, healing, and human connection. As a neurodivergent individual, my journey through psychology and neuroscience studies had always been both personal and academic. I saw patterns that others often missed. I sensed that emotions were not isolated storms but interconnected ecosystems — dynamic, sacred environments that, when stewarded wisely, could heal individuals, relationships, and even societies. Anchorwork™ is the integration of that vision: a framework for emotional stewardship that blends the rigor of science with the reverence of sacred responsibility. It is grounded in systems theory, attachment research, trauma science, and the lived human experience of navigating emotional storms with compassion and hope. I chose nautical language — ships, anchors, storms, and safe harbors — not only because of my father’s legacy as a Navy Commander during the Vietnam War, but because these metaphors perfectly capture what it means to be human: to sail across uncertain waters, to endure storms, and to seek safe harbor in one another. Anchorwork™ was born from the deepest grief, but it sails on the strongest hope — that by learning to steward our emotional ecosystems, we can create a kinder, steadier, more resilient world. This work is dedicated to my father, to every Captain steering their ship through difficult seas, and to every soul learning, even in the fiercest winds, to hold fast to love, to courage, and to their sacred self. May Anchorwork serve as a lighthouse for those who are ready to navigate, heal, and anchor themselves — not in the absence of storms, but in the wisdom to sail through them. — Rebecca Morris Founder, Anchorwork™
