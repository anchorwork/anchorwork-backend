Rebecca Morris Independent Researcher | Founder of Anchorwork™ stewardship@theanchorwork.com www.theanchorwork.com May 13, 2025 Editorial Board Journal of Humanistic Psychology SAGE Publications Dear Editors I am pleased to submit my manuscript titled “Anchorwork™ and the Remapping of Emotional Life, Culture, and Inner Systems” for your consideration. This paper introduces Anchorwork™, which is a narrative and somatic framework that helps individuals navigate grief, neurodivergence, legacy trauma, and emotional fragmentation using metaphor, ritual, and relational pacing. Anchorwork™ draws from trauma-informed care, polyvagal theory, systems psychology, narrative identity work, and intercultural healing traditions. It reimagines internal experience using symbolic structures such as Storms, Crew, Anchors, and Legacy Cargo to support emotional coherence without relying on diagnosis or pathologizing language. The paper also introduces the Anchorwork Outcomes and Resilience Scale (AORS), a polyvagal-aligned measurement tool for symbolic regulation, internal leadership, and legacy repair. The Journal of Humanistic Psychology is an ideal fit for this work given its focus on integrative theory, lived experience, and systems that honor emotional truth. Thank you for your time and consideration. I would be honored to contribute to your journal and welcome any questions. Sincerely Rebecca Morris
