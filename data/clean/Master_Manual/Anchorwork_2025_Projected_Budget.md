Anchorwork™ – Independent Project Tax Status Statement I, Rebecca Morris, am the sole creator and steward of Anchorwork™, a volunteer-led public impact project. Anchorwork™ is not a registered 501(c)(3) and does not currently have a Giscal sponsor. I operate this initiative independently as a grassroots, BIPOC-led project grounded in trauma-informed practice, emotional ritual, and healing justice. All funds received will be used solely for direct programmatic purposes, including the creation and distribution of emotional literacy tools, development of community ritual pilots, and the open-source sharing of supportive resources.
