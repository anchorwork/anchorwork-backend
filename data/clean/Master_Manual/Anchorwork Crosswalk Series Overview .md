Anchorwork™ Crosswalk Guide Mapping Anchorwork’s metaphors, tools, and rituals to major therapeutic & educational frameworks 1. Internal Family Systems (IFS) Core IFS Principles: •Self-leadership is central •All parts are welcome: Protectors, Exiles, Firefighters •Healing occurs through Self-to-part relationship Anchorwork Parallels: •Captain = Self •Crew Members = Parts (Inner Child, Critic, Protector, Saboteur, etc.) •Deck Meetings = Inner dialogues / IFS unblending •Inner Mutiny = Blending or flooding •Parts Mapping = Crew Roster Worksheet •Compassion Rituals = Self-to-part healing work Integration Tips: •Use Crew Mapping worksheets during parts sessions •Replace “blending” with “crew takeover” language •Anchor Drop can be used before or after inner parts work for grounding 2. Dialectical Behavior Therapy (DBT) Core DBT Skills: •Distress tolerance, emotion regulation, mindfulness •Crisis management via diary cards and skills tracking Anchorwork Parallels: •Anchor Activation Tracker = DBT diary card alternative •Storm Recovery Time Tracker = Measures regulation and resilience •Weather Forecast Log = Anticipates emotional triggers (chain analysis alternative) •Crew Conflict Mediation = Skillful interpersonal regulation •Safe Harbor Planning = Coping ahead + creating spaces for rest Integration Tips: •Use metaphor language for emotional education (“You’re in a storm, what anchor can we drop?”) •Replace rigid skills language with accessible metaphors (e.g., distress = “high winds,” anchor = “breathwork”) •Use Storm Tracker to illustrate distress tolerance growth over time 3. Acceptance & Commitment Therapy (ACT) Core ACT Elements: •Values-based living •Acceptance of internal experiences •Committed action in service of purpose Anchorwork Parallels: •Compass Alignment Audit = ACT’s values clarification •Climate Driver Scorecard = Psychological flexibility + lifestyle awareness •Anchor Drop = Grounding in present moment •Fog Mapping = Cognitive defusion •Captain’s Leadership Practice = Self-as-context (wise observer) Integration Tips: •Use compass metaphors in place of abstract values work •Teach clients to notice “climate shifts” rather than judge discomfort •Invite weekly reflection using the Compass + Crew model to guide action choices 4. Social-Emotional Learning (SEL) Core SEL Competencies: •Self-awareness, self-regulation, relationship skills, decision-making Anchorwork Parallels: •Emotional Weather Reports = Mood literacy for youth •Crew Support Inventory = Building relational safety •Anchor Toolbox = Self-regulation kit •Forecast Accuracy Log = Encourages emotional prediction and reflection •Captain/Crew Games = Classroom adaptations for perspective-taking Integration Tips: •Use weather charts in daily classroom routines •Encourage “anchor of the week” activities (e.g., breathwork, music, gratitude) •Turn Anchorwork tools into visual learning aids and check-ins for school counselors 5. Trauma-Informed Care Core Principles: •Safety, trust, choice, empowerment, cultural respect •Understand trauma’s impact on regulation and relational capacity Anchorwork Parallels: •Safe Harbor Rituals = Establishing inner and outer safety •Crew Mapping = Identifies parts shaped by trauma •Legacy Cargo Log = Intergenerational trauma reflection •Climate Driver Analysis = Contextualizes behavioral responses •Anchor Drop = Immediate regulation tool for activation Integration Tips: •Introduce weather metaphors early to normalize emotional fluctuation •Use Anchorwork rituals for group grounding or closure •Empower clients to name “stormy moments” and identify their own anchor strategies 6. Narrative Therapy Core Tenets: •Problems are separate from people •Re-authoring life stories •Honoring culture, values, and meaning-making Anchorwork Parallels: •Storm Naming = Externalizing events and emotions •Captain’s Log Journaling = Narrative re-authoring •Legacy Cargo Sorting = Reframing inherited beliefs •Crew Dialogues = Witnessing multiple inner voices with dignity •Anchor Rituals = Symbolic action for change Integration Tips: •Invite clients to name their life chapters with Anchorwork metaphors •Use Captain/Crew imagery to represent tension between self and systems •Turn rituals (e.g., letting go of cargo) into narrative turning points 7. Somatic Therapy / Polyvagal Theory Core Ideas: •Safety is felt in the body •Co-regulation and self-regulation matter •Autonomic states can be tracked and influenced Anchorwork Parallels: •Anchor Drop = Vagal-toning practice •Storm Recovery Tracker = Tracks arousal, collapse, recovery •Crew Somatic Mapping = “Where does each part live in the body?” •Safe Harbor Visualization = Builds felt sense of safety •Captain’s Body Posture = Somatic cue for leadership state Integration Tips: •Pair every parts-based insight with a body cue check-in •Use breathing, movement, or grounding rituals as “anchor drops” to return to ventral vagal safety •Normalize shifting nervous states as weather patterns, not pathology 8. Mindfulness-Based Interventions Core Practices: •Present-moment awareness •Non-judgmental observation of thought and emotion •Compassion for self and others Anchorwork Parallels: •Anchor Drop = Mindfulness anchor (breath, sensation, mantra) •Watcher Role / Crew Check-In = Awareness without attachment •Captain’s Wheel Reflection = Returning to centered awareness •Fog Mapping = Watching thought spirals and gently redirecting •Compass Practice = Aligning attention with intention Integration Tips: •Introduce “emotional weather forecasts” as mindfulness journaling prompts •Teach clients to anchor through senses or breath when in fog or storm states •Use metaphors to make mindfulness more approachable, especially for youth or trauma survivors Closing Reflection The Anchorwork™ Crosswalk Guide bridges metaphor and methodology — transforming emotional healing into a structured, adaptable, and measurable process that complements (not replaces) established frameworks.
