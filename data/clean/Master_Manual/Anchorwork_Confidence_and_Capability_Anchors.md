Anchorwork Method: Confidence & Capability Anchors This section of the Anchorwork Method helps you move from self-doubt to grounded confidence, from rejection to resilience, and from fear of failure to forward momentum. These tools are designed for moments when belief in yourself feels shaky, and for seasons when you're building toward sustainable success. Anchor Practice 5: Confidence Builder Ritual Use When: You need to believe in yourself again — even just a little.Goal: Reconnect to capability, consistency, and progress. Steps:1. Reflect on wins: What did I do this week that required effort or courage?2. Affirm your becoming: “Even when I’m doubting, I’m still building.”3. Visualize success: Picture your future self doing what you hope to do — job, fatherhood, partnership.4. Say one thing you believe you’re good at — and one you’re working on.5. End with action: Name one small thing you’ll do this week to grow. Anchor Practice 6: Strength + Skill Growth Grid Use When: You’re not sure what to focus on or feel “behind.”Goal: Identify current strengths + growth zones and take concrete action.
