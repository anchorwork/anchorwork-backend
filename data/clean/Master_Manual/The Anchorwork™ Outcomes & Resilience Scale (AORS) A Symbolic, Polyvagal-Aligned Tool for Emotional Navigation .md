The Anchorwork™ Outcomes & Resilience Scale (AORS): A Symbolic, Polyvagal-Aligned Tool for Emotional Navigation Abstract The Anchorwork™ Outcomes & Resilience Scale (AORS) is a metaphor-based, trauma- informed measurement tool designed to assess emotional coherence, regulation, and values alignment in individuals navigating complex emotional landscapes. Grounded in Polyvagal Theory, narrative psychology, and symbolic ritual, AORS provides a qualitative and accessible way to track emotional literacy, grounding capacity, co-regulation, and legacy healing. The scale avoids clinical pathologizing by offering symbolic metaphors such as “storm,” “anchor,” and “safe harbor” to support emotional tracking across diverse cultural, neurodivergent, and trauma- informed contexts. AORS is currently in early-stage development, with an open call for research partnerships, clinical pilot opportunities, and collaborative validation. 1. Introduction Despite advances in trauma-informed care and somatic psychology, many existing assessment tools rely on either clinical language or symptom-focused frameworks that can reinforce shame or misattunement. The Anchorwork™ Outcomes & Resilience Scale (AORS) was developed to fill this gap. It uses metaphor-rich, consent-centered language to help individuals identify, articulate, and regulate their emotional states while promoting polyvagal-aligned nervous system awareness. Anchorwork is a framework for emotional navigation that sees the self as a symbolic vessel: the hull (body), crew (inner parts and external support), anchor (grounding rituals), compass (core values), and cargo (inherited emotional patterns). The AORS distills these elements into 16 practical, trackable statements covering emotional awareness, regulation, relational support, and legacy processing. 2. Structure and Domains of the AORS The AORS includes 16 items divided into four domains: ➢ Emotional Awareness & Literacy: ability to name internal states and track emotional climates ➢ Regulation & Grounding: ability to use symbolic anchors or rituals to restore calm ➢ Connection & Co-Regulation: ability to access support, name needs, and feel emotionally witnessed ➢ Legacy Healing & Values Alignment: awareness of inherited emotional patterns and capacity to act in alignment with personal values Each item is scored from 1 (Not at all true) to 5 (Completely true). Total scores offer a symbolic “emotional weather report,” and are accompanied by reflection prompts for deeper narrative integration. 3. Polyvagal Integration and Symbolic Language The AORS translates polyvagal-informed concepts into metaphorical, emotionally safe language: ➢ Anchor = ventral vagal grounding practices ➢ Storm = sympathetic activation or emotional overwhelm ➢ Stillwater = parasympathetic recovery ➢ Safe Harbor = co-regulation or emotional safety with others This symbolic approach reduces reliance on clinical jargon while still aligning with nervous system science. The tool encourages body-based pacing, sensory awareness, and cultural responsiveness in both self-reflection and facilitated use. 4. Applications and Use Contexts The AORS is designed for flexible use across a range of settings, including: ➢ 1:1 therapy and coaching ➢ Community or group ritual practice ➢ Peer-led reflection in non-clinical environment Because of its narrative and somatic design, it complements ACT, DBT, narrative therapy, and IFS. It is especially resonant in BIPOC, LGBTQ+, and spiritually integrated healing communities. 5. Research Pathways and Future Vision Anchorwork is currently seeking research partners to validate the AORS through clinical trials, community-based research, or qualitative outcome studies. The scale may be particularly useful in assessing: ➢ Narrative coherence over time ➢ Regulation patterns in neurodivergent populations ➢ Co-regulation outcomes in grief and relational repair Future iterations may include child, couple, and digital tool versions, as well as integration into polyvagal-aligned AI support systems.
