Anchorwork Founder Protection Starter Kit 1. Trademark Checklist - Trademark the name 'The Anchorwork Method™' as a service mark (therapy, wellness, coaching). - Trademark tiered names: Anchorwork Home™, Anchorwork Care™, Anchorwork Teams™, Anchorwork Pro™, Anchorbot™. - Trademark logo, tagline, and visual brand elements (if applicable). - Use USPTO.gov for submission or consult a trademark attorney. - File as 'Intent-to-Use' if platform is not yet launched. 2. Copyright Checklist - Copyright core documents: scripts, rituals, emotional repair plans, and caregiver tools. - Copyright brand story and visual materials if uniquely illustrated or stylized. - Register on copyright.gov (approx. $45 per submission). - Maintain date-stamped drafts to prove originality. 3.
