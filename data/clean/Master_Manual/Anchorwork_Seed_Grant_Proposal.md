Anchorwork™: A Symbolic System for Emotional Healing and Neurodivergent Empowerment Seed Grant Proposal Executive Summary Anchorwork™ is a trauma-informed, metaphor-based emotional support system designed for neurodivergent adults and parents who often feel emotionally misattuned, misdiagnosed, or culturally unsupported. Through symbolic rituals, somatic literacy, and collective storytelling, Anchorwork™ empowers people to name their emotional weather, navigate inherited trauma, and build inner coherence without relying on diagnostic frameworks. This proposal seeks seed funding to develop key components, run grassroots pilots, and collaborate with early adopters and community stewards. The Problem Most mental health systems are still designed for neurotypical, verbal, and performance-based emotional models. As a result:- Neurodivergent adults often receive treatment that doesn't match their sensory, emotional, or pacing needs.- Parents—especially those breaking intergenerational cycles—are left without tools to support themselves or their children emotionally without shame.- Emotional systems over-focus on symptom management and under-resource ritual, metaphor, and narrative repair—especially powerful for non-linear thinkers and feelers. The Anchorwork™ Solution Anchorwork™ offers a new model: a living emotional ecosystem rooted in story, structure, and return. Using universal metaphors like the storm, ship, compass, crew, and anchor, it allows participants to:- Regulate without suppression- Understand parts of self without pathology- Grieve and repair inherited legacies through symbolic ritualsPractices include Weather Check-ins, Crew Mapping, Anchor Rituals, and Legacy Cargo Ceremonies—delivered through journaling, visual tools, breathwork, and communal scaffolding. Target Community - Neurodivergent adults seeking structure that honors their rhythm- Parents healing from emotional disconnection or intergenerational trauma- People underserved by clinical or diagnostic systems but hungry for ritual, story, and embodied careInitial pilots will focus on peer-led groups, educators, and caregiving communities—with strong potential for scale into schools, support networks, and online accessibility. Use of Seed Funds A $5K–$10K grant would allow me to:- Design and test 1–2 core Anchorwork™ toolkits- Run 1–2 small group pilots with 5–10 participants each (free to them)- Collaborate with advisors, parents, and ND adults to co-adapt language and pacing- Compensate myself part-time for 8–12 weeks of dedicated development- Develop a digital Notion-based ritual guidebook and printable decks- Begin building the Anchorwork Outcomes & Resilience Scale (AORS) as a research tool Impact Goals Short-term:- Increased emotional literacy using weather/crew metaphors- Ritual use becomes part of daily regulation- Legacy patterns begin to be named and rewrittenLong-term:- Anchorwork™ becomes a widely used tool in ND-affirming, non-clinical healing spaces- Community-based models of ritual return and co-regulation emerge- Evaluation data contributes to shifting emotional care culture from symptom to symbol My Story as the Creator I am a neurodivergent, multi-role caregiver and cultural worker who has spent years navigating emotional fragmentation without a map. I created Anchorwork™ not as an abstract system—but as a lifeline, a vessel, and a language I needed but couldn’t find. It is the culmination of my lived experience, personal grief work, embodied training, and deep study across trauma theory, narrative identity, somatics, and cross-cultural emotional systems.I’ve carried this map quietly for years. I’m now ready—and deeply committed—to share it, research it, and scale it with care. Closing This is not a program. It is a practice of return.Anchorwork™ gives people a way to navigate emotion without shame, to grieve without collapse, and to lead their own lives with more clarity, rhythm, and ritual.I’m not backed by an institution. I’m backed by a vision—and a community that’s waiting for a map that feels like home.
