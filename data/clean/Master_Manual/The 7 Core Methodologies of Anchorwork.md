The 7 Core Methodologies of Anchorwork™ Anchorwork™ is more than a healing philosophy — it is a full, integrated system of emotional navigation, trauma recovery, and resilience-building. These seven core methodologies form the foundation of the Anchorwork model, guiding individuals, families, communities, and practitioners through every kind of emotional weather. 1. Emotional Navigation & Climate Theory Metaphor: Weather patterns, ships, and emotional ecosystems Purpose: To name, normalize, and navigate emotion without judgment Includes: •Emotional Weather vs. Climate •Personal climate types (e.g. Fog-Dense, Heated, Sky- Wide) •Navigation Influences (Currents, Winds, Fog, Legacy Cargo) •Seasonal cycles and emotional forecasting •Tools: Captain’s Log, Climate Maps, Compass Rituals 2. Habit Healing & Kindness-Based Rewiring Metaphor: Steering, conditioning, and ritualized behavior Purpose: To rewire survival patterns through compassion and positive reinforcement Includes: •Classical & operant conditioning (trauma-informed) •Rituals for habit repair and nervous system repatterning •Drop Anchor, Dock Break, and Anchor Pairing •Tools: AORS resilience scale, Habit Healing Tracker 3. Nervous System Regulation & Somatic Stewardship Metaphor: Anchors, Safe Harbors, and Inner Maintenance Purpose: To restore physiological safety through grounding and trauma-informed care Includes: •Polyvagal-aligned tools (sympathetic, dorsal, ventral states) •Sensory regulation & restorative pacing •Lantern Keeper Rituals and body-based grief tending •Tools: Daily Ritual Templates, Regulation Logs 4. Communication & Relational Stewardship Metaphor: Crew dynamics, Open Decks, Storm Loops Purpose: To transform relational conflict into co-regulation and emotional literacy Includes: •The ANCHOR Model (Attunement, Non-Judgment, Compassion, Hope, Openness, Responsibility) •Safe Harbor dialogue tools •Inner Crew check-ins (parts integration) •Tools: Repair Ritual Scripts, Anchor Phrases, Dialogue Cards 5. Collective Trauma & Systemic Climate Healing Metaphor: Emotional climate collapse, fleet navigation, ancestral weather systems Purpose: To address grief, injustice, and burnout at the community, cultural, and ecological level Includes: •Collective Emotional Climate Mapping •Community Mourning & Intergenerational Rituals •Eco-anxiety and activist burnout care •Tools: Group Forecast Check-Ins, Climate Grief Circle Guides 6. Sacred Biodiversity & Emotional Inclusion Metaphor: Human emotion as ecological diversity Purpose: To affirm and protect the full range of human neurotypes, sensory styles, and feeling languages Includes: •Emotional and cognitive biodiversity •Adaptive masking and Chameleon Climate unmasking •Sensory-specific anchors and identity ritualization •Tools: Crew Diversity Reflections, Adaptive Anchor Menus, Inclusion Language Guide 7. Measurement, Integration & Ethical Expansion Metaphor: Star Charts, Resilience Mapping, and Stewardship Agreements Purpose: To track progress, scale ethically, and translate Anchorwork into clinical, digital, and communal contexts Includes: •AORS 16-item scale (self + facilitator) •Qualitative + quantitative measurement integration •Program evaluation and impact dashboards •Tools: Implementation Blueprints, Consent Protocols, Role-Based Modules
