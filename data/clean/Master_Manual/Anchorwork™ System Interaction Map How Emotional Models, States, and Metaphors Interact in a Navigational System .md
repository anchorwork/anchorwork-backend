Purpose The Anchorwork™ System Interaction Map is designed to visually and conceptually show how different components of emotional life—trauma patterns, psychological models, nervous system states, inherited beliefs, and metaphor-based tools—interact dynamically within a single ecosystem. Unlike traditional models that silo emotions or disciplines, Anchorwork™ reveals the interdependence between somatic, narrative, and symbolic systems. II.
