Seasonal Ritual Kit: Rebecca & Rick’s Emotional Reset Guide This ritual kit offers season-based ways to reconnect, slow down, and rebuild emotional presence. Each season includes grounding rituals, creative moments, and intimacy resets. Spring – Reawakening & Clarity • Host a 'Truth Garden' night. Plant herbs or flowers while sharing one truth you’ve been holding back. • Play the 'What’s Growing?' game: each share one internal thing you’re working on and how the other can support it. • Walk barefoot in the grass while holding hands. Practice silent connection and slow breath. • Clean out one drawer or closet together while letting go of one past wound as a symbol. Summer – Joy, Energy, and Intimacy • Build a grilled meal together. Choose each other's favorite ingredients. • Set up a backyard movie or video game night. Share snacks, laughs, and zero pressure. • Host a 'Fire Night': burn paper notes of fear or frustration, then write one loving vow in return. • Reignite touch: 15-minute 'slow hands' massage with music and silence. No words, just presence. Autumn – Grief, Grounding & Gratitude • Create a memory altar for Rebecca’s dad or other losses. Light candles and speak their names. • Go on a drive without music. Let silence reveal what’s underneath. • Each person writes down 3 things they’re grateful for about the other—and reads them aloud under a blanket. • Bake something from scratch together. Let the kitchen be therapy. Winter – Stillness, Reflection, and Softness • Host a 'Candle Night': turn off all lights, light candles, and ask: 'Where is your heart tonight?' • Set up a weekly tea or cocoa ritual. Ask each other: 'What do you need this week to feel safe?' • Practice 'Intentional Silence': Sit together for 10 minutes, hand in hand, no phones. • Share a playlist and each pick one song that reminds you of each other—listen while cuddling.
