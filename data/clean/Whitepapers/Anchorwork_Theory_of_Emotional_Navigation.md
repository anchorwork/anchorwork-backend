The Anchorwork Theory of Emotional Navigation By Rebecca Morris© 2024 The Anchorwork Method™ Introduction The Anchorwork Theory of Emotional Navigation is a trauma-informed, emotionally intelligent psychological framework designed by Rebecca Morris. It provides individuals, couples, families, caregivers, and leaders with a tangible metaphor for navigating internal emotional states and relational dynamics. Drawing from depth psychology, attachment theory, somatic practices, and narrative healing, this method translates complexity into clarity, giving people tools to reconnect with themselves and others in real time. The Ship as Self Anchorwork uses the metaphor of a ship to represent the self. Each part of the ship corresponds to a psychological component: - The Hull: the unconscious, where trauma and deep instincts live- The Cabin: the subconscious, memory and attachment stories- The Deck: the present self navigating daily life (ego)- The Sails: the ideal self and guiding values (superego)- The Wheel: agency and chosen action- The Rudder: outside influences and triggers- The Stern: the past still shaping movement- The Bow: the future being steered toward- The Anchor: grounding rituals, values, and emotional regulation practices Emotional Modes: Fire, Fog, Freeze, and Flow The Anchorwork Theory introduces a real-time emotional state system that helps users identify and respond to their current regulation mode:- Fire: Overwhelm, defensiveness, or panic (fight mode)- Fog: Shutdown, confusion, or detachment (dissociation or shutdown)- Freeze: Stillness with internal chaos, immobilization- Flow: Present, safe, emotionally available and integratedEach mode has corresponding rituals, scripts, and co-regulation strategies to restore balance and connection. Applications of Anchorwork Anchorwork is designed for use across life contexts:- Individuals: self-awareness, healing rituals, emotional regulation- Couples and Co-Parents: repair tools, shared reflection, and crisis recovery- Families: neurodivergent parenting, co-regulation, emotional mapping- Caregiving: dementia support, grief processing, relational presence- Teams and Leaders: conflict repair, emotional safety, resilience training- Therapists and Facilitators: client-guided emotional scaffolding and relational anchoring Real-Time AI Companion Support The Anchorwork Method includes a built-in AI emotional companion, trained in tone-matching, emotional reflection, and ritual recommendation. This AI adapts to user behavior, chosen voice (Compassionate Friend, Calm Coach, Soulful Guide), and state of regulation. It acts as a steady, nonjudgmental co-pilot for emotional reflection, repair, and resilience-building. Core Philosophical Values Anchorwork is grounded in radical compassion, sovereignty, trauma-awareness, and shared dignity. It assumes that every person has an internal navigation system, and that with the right scaffolding, anyone can return to the wheel and steer their own ship. The method honors neurodivergence, relational rupture, and the lifelong work of emotional literacy as a path toward connection and healing.
