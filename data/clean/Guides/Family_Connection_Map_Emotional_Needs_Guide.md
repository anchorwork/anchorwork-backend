Family Connection Map: Visual Guide to Emotional Safety & Needs This is a visual-style relationship map designed to help your family understand one another’s emotional needs, triggers, communication styles, and calming tools. Rebecca – The Core & Creator • Strengths: Empathic, passionate, sensory intelligent, deeply creative. • Struggles: Overstimulation, rejection sensitivity, overfunctioning. • Communication needs: Reflection, affirmation, follow-through. • Comforts: Soft tones, low lights, space to create, presence over performance. Rick – The Anchor & Quiet Protector • Strengths: Calm, thoughtful, helpful under pressure, loyal. • Struggles: Emotional expression, avoidance, internalized fear of failure. • Communication needs: No pressure. Questions with time to respond. • Comforts: Structure, praise, quiet companionship, non-verbal affection. Anneliese – The Visionary & Deep Feeler (ADHD) • Strengths: Insightful, poetic, thoughtful, mature. • Struggles: Motivation cycles, internal chaos, sensitivity to stress. • Support needs: Structure with freedom. Time to transition. Emotional check-ins. • Comforts: Gentle attention, journaling space, flexible routines. Juliet – The Spark & Soundtrack (ADHD) • Strengths: Expressive, funny, charismatic, smart. • Struggles: Emotional dysregulation, fast speech, interrupting. • Support needs: Sensory regulation. Validated feelings. Gentle redirection. • Comforts: Noise-canceling headphones, cuddles, playful teaching moments. Ella – The Quiet Force & Heart Mirror (Autism) • Strengths: Sensitive, intuitive, affectionate on her terms. • Struggles: Routines, transitions, sensory processing. • Support needs: Visual cues, short instructions, non-verbal communication. • Comforts: Weighted items, predictability, songs, laying close to Mom.
