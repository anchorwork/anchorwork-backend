Why This Matters: Grounds the reader in the language, tone, and pacing of emotional stewardship. Without it, readers may try to “fix” rather than feel. What Makes It Unique: Immediately replaces clinical or cognitive frameworks with metaphor, narrative, and consent-based ritual. Establishes emotional safety as structure, not suggestion.
