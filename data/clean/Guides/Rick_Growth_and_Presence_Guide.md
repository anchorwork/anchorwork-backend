Rick's Growth & Presence Guide Your Role Is Not to Fix Her – It's to Be With Her - Rebecca doesn’t expect perfection. She needs presence. - Show up. Stay curious. Speak even when it's uncomfortable. - Your silence says more than you think. Engage with care. What Rebecca Needs Most From You - Emotional initiation (start conversations, check in, affirm). - Physical comfort without pressure (touch her hand, hold her in silence). - Action-based love: remember her favorites, participate in her rituals. - Share your thoughts, even if unsure. Silence feels like rejection. Your Own Healing Matters Too - Your emotional passivity may come from childhood survival. That’s okay. - Explore your identity outside of Rebecca and as her partner. - Journal. Read. Reflect. Ask: ‘What do I want? What do I feel?’ - Consider therapy, shadow work, or mindfulness practices to reconnect with your emotional world. What to Practice Weekly - One loving text, handwritten note, or verbal appreciation. - One moment of emotional initiation (asking how she feels or offering your thoughts first). - One shared ritual: a walk, a playlist, a meal together with no distractions. - Ask Rebecca: ‘How can I love you better this week?’ and listen fully. Prompts to Ask Yourself When Things Feel Hard - Am I present or avoiding? - Is this silence protecting me or pushing her away? - What might Rebecca be feeling underneath her words? - How can I show care instead of shutting down? Mantras for Building Confidence - ‘Showing up imperfectly is better than disappearing silently.’ - ‘My presence matters.’ - ‘Being emotionally available doesn’t mean being weak.’ - ‘I can grow into the partner I want to be. Every step counts.’
