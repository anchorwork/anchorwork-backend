Quick Reference: How to Love and Support Rebecca Her Core Needs - To be understood, not fixed. - Gentle tone, steady presence, soft eyes. - Engagement, not avoidance. - Memory and effort shown through action—not just words. Big Triggers - Silence during emotional moments. - Dismissiveness, being told she’s overreacting. - Lack of effort or detachment during conflict. - Being expected to carry the emotional load alone. Sensory Comforts - Room-temp Dr. Pepper (always). - Nest candles: Rose Noir & Oud, Sea Salt, Driftwood. - Bella Canvas / Comfort Colors shirts only. - Jazz music, The Piano Guys, soft lighting, minimal clutter. - Clean blankets, soft textures, warm smells. When She's Spiraling - Sit close. Say: 'You’re safe. I’m here. I’m not going anywhere.' - Wrap her in a blanket. Offer her favorite music or drink. - Use grounding statements: 'You are okay. I see you. I love you.' - Do not walk away. Do not get defensive. Do not go silent. Ways to Show Love (Without Her Asking) - Text or write a small note: 'I’m proud of you. You carry so much.' - Plan a sensory-friendly date: grilled dinner, music, silence, candles. - Gift her something in her aesthetic (cats, florals, textures, scent). - Ask her opinion. Listen. Care. Ask follow-up questions. - Hold her without needing her to calm down first. Sex & Emotional Intimacy - She desires to feel emotionally connected *before* intimacy. - She finds pleasure in presence and dominance. Ask with curiosity. - Say: 'You’re powerful. I love watching you take control.' - Never withhold, dismiss, or ignore her desire—it’s an emotional bridge.
