Anchorwork™ Outcomes & Resilience Scale (AORS) Form and Instructions for Self, Facilitator, or Group Use Overview The Anchorwork™ Outcomes & Resilience Scale (AORS) is a metaphor-safe, trauma-informed tool designed to assess emotional literacy, grounding capacity, relational support access, legacy processing, and values-based action. It uses accessible, symbolic language to reduce shame, support self- awareness, and offer consistent reflection without triggering clinical or diagnostic defenses. It may be used: • As a self-assessment (daily, weekly, or monthly) • By a facilitator or therapist to track growth over time • With groups to assess collective emotional climate Each item is rated on a 1–5 scale and can be paired with reflection prompts. Terminology You Need to Know To understand and use AORS effectively, it helps to be familiar with the following Anchorwork™ terms: • Emotional Climate: The overall emotional atmosphere a person is experiencing (e.g., Fog-Dense, Storm Season, Sky- Wide). Different from momentary feelings—climates reflect patterns. • Weather Language: Metaphoric words used to describe feelings (e.g., “I feel scattered like wind,” or “heavy like rain”). Allows emotional naming without judgment. • Drop Anchor: A somatic or symbolic practice that helps bring the nervous system into regulation. Could be breath, touch, movement, or a ritual pause. • Safe Harbor: A person or place that brings emotional safety, co-regulation, and calm. Sometimes also a mindset or setting. • Compass: A person’s values, used to guide decisions during emotional storms. Compass Check rituals reconnect the user with purpose and integrity. • Star Chart: A longer-term map of hopes, dreams, and purpose. Used to orient during loss, uncertainty, or chronic grief. • Legacy Cargo: Emotional beliefs, behaviors, and stories inherited from family, culture, or systems. Some are helpful (resilience), others are heavy (guilt, fear, shame). • Crew: Inner parts (IFS-informed) and external people who offer support, reflection, or guidance. • Forecasting: The practice of naming your current climate and emotional trends. Used in group or family settings to prepare and co-regulate. Instructions Respond to each item below by selecting a number from 1 to 5 that reflects how true this statement has felt for you in the past 7 days (or since your last check-in). 1 = Not at all true 2 = A little true 3 = Somewhat true 4 = Mostly true 5 = Completely true You may complete this on your own or with the support of a trained facilitator. Optional reflection prompts are provided for deeper insight. The 16 AORS Items A. Climate Awareness & Emotional Literacy 1. I can name my emotional state using weather-based language (e.g., foggy, stormy, scattered). 2. I can describe the current “emotional climate” I’m experiencing (e.g., tense, clear, flooded). 3. I notice when my emotions are shifting and can track those changes over time. 4. I feel less shame or confusion about having strong or changing emotions. B. Regulation & Grounding 5. I have at least one ritual or tool (e.g., Drop Anchor, breathwork) that helps me return to calm. 6. I can calm my body or nervous system during difficult moments. 7. I recover more quickly than I used to after emotional overwhelm. 8. I am able to pause or slow down when I feel overstimulated. C. Connection & Co-Regulation 9. I know who in my life feels like a Safe Harbor (a person who helps me regulate). 10. I feel more able to name what I need from others when I’m struggling. 11. I have at least one person I can emotionally forecast with (share climate check-ins). 12. I feel less alone inside my emotional landscape. D. Legacy Healing & Value Alignment 13. I am becoming more aware of emotional patterns or beliefs I may have inherited. 14. I feel empowered to release or rewrite emotional stories that no longer serve me. 15. I can name values that guide my actions (e.g., compassion, courage). 16. I am acting more often in alignment with my values, even during stress. Scoring Guide (Optional) Add up all 16 item scores to calculate your AORS total. Total possible score: 80 • 64–80: Strong emotional awareness and resilience. Reflect on growth and deepen your anchor rituals. • 48–63: Moderate resilience. Reinforce your rituals, relationships, and reflection. • 32–47: Vulnerable emotional climate. Consider support and consistent use of Anchorwork tools. • Below 32: Storm Season. Seek Safe Harbor. Re-anchor and prioritize gentle support. Scores are not diagnostic and should never be used to judge or label. They are a symbolic weather report of your current state, meant to support emotional visibility, pacing, and ritual adjustment. Optional Reflection Questions • What climate(s) have I noticed most this week? • Which ritual(s) helped me the most? • What legacy belief, if any, surfaced or shifted? • What value guided one key decision I made? • Who felt like my anchor or Safe Harbor?
